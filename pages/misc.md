### Other (Eclectic) Interests

However, good food is not equivalent to high-quality or expensive food, but food that fills the soul and meets us where we're at, whenever we need it. I love all things improv comedy – I'm not comfortable enough to actually do any improv (though maybe that'll change in the future) but my current favorite improv things are the [Actors on Actors on Actors on Actors Podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CMpGGcnupsw), the [Offbook: The Improvised Musical Podcast](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ivf58k53nu0), and the streaming service [Dropout](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bB1ESv06hkI&t=438s). I also adore the british show [Taskmaster!](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tv_q0RMaV6Y)

I've been playing the piano since I was a kid, and picked up the alto saxophone in middle school. I'm horrendously bad at playing jazz music, but I love listening to jazz, along with video game soundtracks, tango and cumbia, and of course classical music. I studied piano under Reiko Uchida while at Columbia!
